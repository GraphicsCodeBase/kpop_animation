### Patterns worth noticing (to discuss)
- **The switch is in the structure:** the first half is mostly *suits* (studio, reception, palace); the second half switches to *streetwear* (rotunda, balls, arched room). Check whether that matches where the song changes.

- **Colour-coding the duo:** in S5 each member gets her own background colour (blue vs. pink).
- **Day vs. night worlds:** dark studio and amber reception vs. the bright pool and white palace.

### Two zones
**Zone A: the pool basin** (main dance area)
- Rectangular empty pool. Flat floor and walls covered in **small mosaic tiles**, a mix of aqua, light blue and white.
- **Dark navy lane lines** across the floor (pool lane markings), reading as strong diagonals in the high-angle shot.
- **Pool stairs** in the back-left corner: cream steps with **orange nosing stripes**, chrome handrail.

**Zone B: curved mosaic stairs** (close-ups and the stair performance)
- Sunken channels with **curved walls**, thick rounded **cream coping** on top.
- Finer blue-and-white mosaic, stairs rising toward the back, chrome handrail along the wall.
- May be a separate location from Zone A, joined in the edit.

### Props (Zone A)

### Light & colour
- **Lighting:** high-key, soft and diffuse (feels like a big skylight or overcast daylight). Very soft shadows; mainly soft contact shadows under feet and props.
- **Palette:** aqua/white base covers most of the frame · navy lines for structure · **warm accents** (coral lounger, red booth, orange seats and stair stripes) pop against the cool base · pastels (balloons, flags) for playfulness.
- **Grade:** soft and slightly desaturated, lifted blacks, cool and airy.

### Camera
- **Wide:** high angle from the pool edge looking down at about 45°. Shows the floor lines, props, and both dancers.
- **Medium and close-up:** near eye level, close to the dancer; motion blur on fast moves.

### Styling seen here
- **Dahyun:** white track jacket with navy stripes, denim vest, white trousers, gold chain.
- **Chaeyoung:** oversized white shirt, grey patterned top, light-wash wide jeans, white wristband.
