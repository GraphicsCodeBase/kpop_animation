- Claude's frame observations (7 stills sampled at 0:04, 0:18, 0:40, 1:10, 1:45, 2:20, 2:55; no audio, no motion):
  - **Concept shown through structure:** the title is "Switch to me", and the MV literally keeps switching sets and outfits. The idea isn't just in the lyrics; it shapes the edit.
  - **Sets as contrasting colour worlds:** a dark, low-key studio with a single magenta balloon / bright blue-and-white mosaic staircase / dim retro room with a CRT TV playing beach footage / white marble palace hall with chandelier / flat pink pop-art heart background.
  - **Styling contrast:** matching formal black suits and ties vs. Y2K hip-hop streetwear (red bucket hat, braids, chains, varsity jersey). Streetwear inside the palace is a deliberate clash.
  - **Framing range:** tight close-ups where the artist looks straight into the lens (the ones that make fans feel addressed), a perfectly symmetric wide shot in the palace, a frame-within-a-frame (the TV).
  - **Accent colour:** one saturated object (the magenta balloon) in an otherwise dark frame pulls the eye.
